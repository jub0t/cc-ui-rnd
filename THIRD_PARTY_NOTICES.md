### Why this is compatible with AGPL-3.0

Section 13 of the GPL-3.0 exists for exactly this combination:

> Notwithstanding any other provision of this License, you have permission to
> link or combine any covered work with a work licensed under version 3 of the
> GNU Affero General Public License into a single combined work, and to convey
> the resulting work.

So the combined binary is conveyable. Slint's portion remains GPL-3.0-only,
this project's portion remains AGPL-3.0-or-later, and the AGPL's section 13
network requirement applies to the combination as a whole. AGPL-3.0-or-later
includes AGPL-3.0, which is the version the clause above names.

Anyone forking this tree who would rather not be bound by the GPL must take
Slint under one of its other two licences and remove or replace this project's
AGPL-licensed code accordingly. The two cannot be mixed.

### Slint's own dependencies

Slint pulls in the renderer selected by the feature flags in
`slint/Cargo.toml` — Skia by default, FemtoVG over wgpu under `--features
wgpu` — along with winit and their transitive crates. These are predominantly
MIT/Apache-2.0/BSD licensed. Run `cargo tree` or `cargo license` against
`slint/Cargo.toml` for the resolved set of any given build.

## Note on `.cargo/config.toml`

`-C target-cpu=native` makes binaries built from this tree specific to the
machine that built them. That is a build-portability matter rather than a
licensing one, but it means the binaries here are not the ones to distribute —
see the comments in that file.
